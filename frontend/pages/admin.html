Six things worth doing once. Nothing here ticks because you pressed a button: some steps tick when Ashley can see for itself that they are done, and the rest tick on your say-so, which those steps tell you plainly. Come back whenever you add somebody or move the model.

    People

    Everyone here gets their own face on the sign-in screen, their own private conversations, and their own set of rules. Characters are shared by the whole family. The two tags on each row show which rules that person is on.

    Add someone

    Their colour

    They will type this on the sign-in screen.

    Rules Ashley follows

    Everyone has their own rules. You start from one of four ready-made templates — two for younger children, two for teenagers — and you can rewrite either box for that person alone. The rules are sent to the model with every message, before anything anyone types.

    Whose rules would you like to see?

    The four templates are Ashley's own wording. If you have edited them in the past and want the originals back, this puts every template back to the text Ashley shipped with. It does not touch anything you have written for one person.

    Their template

    Pick the one that fits their age and how much room you want the characters to have. Everything below starts from this.

    A box you have rewritten keeps your wording and ignores the template until you reset it.

    Tone, style, how a character stays in character, and what they do with awkward subjects. Safe to experiment with.

    The safety rules below are the child-safety layer. They come after the character, so they have the last word. Weakening or emptying this box is what lets unsafe replies through. If you are unsure, press "Reset to template" — that puts back the wording this person's template came with.

    Rules that are always on

    Cannot be changed here

    These apply to every person and every template, whatever you write above, and they are added last so they always have the final word. They are deliberately not editable from this page — changing them means editing Ashley's source code.
